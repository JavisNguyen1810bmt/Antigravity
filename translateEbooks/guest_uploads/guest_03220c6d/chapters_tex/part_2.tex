19. Admissions Criteria \& Application Protocol
The MS-DSAI program seeks candidates with exceptional quantitative capabilities and strong programming
fundamentals. Admissions are conducted on a rolling basis across Fall and Spring intakes.
Academic Prerequisites
Bachelor’s Degree in CS, Data Science, Math, Statistics,
Physics, or Engineering.
Minimum Undergraduate GPA: 3.20 on a 4.00 scale.
Prerequisite Coursework: Calculus III, Linear Algebra,
Probability Theory, and Data Structures in C++/Python.
Application Checklist
Official University Transcripts.
3 Letters of Recommendation (Academic/Professional).
Statement of Purpose (1,000 words).
GRE General Test (Optional, recommended quantitative
score > 165).
TOEFL iBT (Min 100) or IELTS (Min 7.5) for non-native
English speakers.

22. Graduation Clearance Checklist
Final Degree Completion Steps:
1. Completion of all 36 required units with a cumulative GPA ≥ 3.00.
2. Successful submission and public oral defense of Thesis / Capstone.
3. Formal clearance from the University Library and Supercomputing Facility.
4. Filing of Graduation Application Form with the Office of the Registrar.
